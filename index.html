A few good questions

Curious about Grydin?

Here are a few quick notes about the shopping experience.

What is Grydin Mart?

Grydin Mart is a grocery-shopping experience designed to bring everyday browsing and ordering together in one place.

How do I find what I need?

Browse the available categories or use search to look for a product, brand, or item on your list.

Can I save items for later?

The shopping experience includes a wishlist, so you can keep track of favourites for another time.

How do I follow my order?

Order updates let you check its progress after placing an order. Delivery options and availability are shown in the shopping experience.
